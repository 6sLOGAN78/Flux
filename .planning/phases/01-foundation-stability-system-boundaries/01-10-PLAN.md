| T-03-04 | Denial of Service | SIGTERM/cleanup | mitigate | Unready-first bounded drain and all-closer aggregation tests |
| T-03-SC | Tampering | Integration dependencies | mitigate | Exact PostgreSQL/Redis pins and Go checksum verification |

|---|---|

| T-02-04 | Repudiation | Migration result | mitigate | Structured start/end version and process outcome without sensitive diagnostics |
| T-02-SC | Tampering | PostgreSQL test image | mitigate | Exact verified image pin inherited from Plan 01 and frozen Go module checksums |
